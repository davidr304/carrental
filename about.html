About DriveRent

We are more than just a car rental company. We are your trusted mobility partner, providing reliable vehicles and exceptional service to make every journey memorable.

Our Mission & Vision

Founded in 2015, DriveRent started with a simple mission: to make car rental accessible, affordable, and hassle-free for everyone. What began as a small fleet of 10 cars has grown into one of the most trusted car rental services with over 500 vehicles across the country.

Our vision is to revolutionize the car rental industry by leveraging technology to provide seamless booking experiences, maintaining the highest standards of vehicle quality, and delivering customer service that exceeds expectations.

Our Journey

Milestones in DriveRent's growth story

2015

Company Founded

DriveRent was founded with just 10 vehicles and a single location in downtown. Our mission was to provide affordable, reliable rental cars with exceptional service.

2017

Expansion to 5 Cities

We expanded our operations to 5 major cities and grew our fleet to 100 vehicles. Introduced our first mobile app for easier booking and management.

2019

Technology Integration

Launched our fully integrated booking platform with real-time availability, digital contracts, and keyless entry technology for select vehicles.

2021

Sustainability Initiative

Introduced our first electric and hybrid vehicles to the fleet. Partnered with environmental organizations to offset our carbon footprint.

2023

National Recognition

Awarded "Best Car Rental Service" by National Travel Association. Expanded to 25 cities with over 500 vehicles including luxury and specialty cars.
