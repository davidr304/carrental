2. Eligibility Requirements

To rent a vehicle from DriveRent, you must meet the following requirements:

Age Requirements

Renters must be at least 21 years old. For luxury or specialty vehicles, the minimum age may be 25. Drivers under 25 may be subject to a Young Driver Surcharge.

Valid Driver's License

You must possess a valid, unexpired driver's license issued by your country of residence. International renters may need an International Driving Permit (IDP) along with their home country license.

Credit/Debit Card

A valid credit or debit card in the renter's name is required for security deposit. The card must have sufficient funds to cover the security deposit and any anticipated charges.

3. Rental Agreement Terms

The rental agreement begins when you take possession of the vehicle and ends when the vehicle is returned to the designated location and inspected by DriveRent staff.

3.1 Rental Period

The rental period is calculated in 24-hour increments from the time of pickup. A 59-minute grace period is allowed for returns. Returns more than one hour late will incur additional charges.

3.2 Extension of Rental

If you wish to extend your rental period, you must contact DriveRent at least 24 hours before the scheduled return time. Extensions are subject to vehicle availability and approval.

3.3 Vehicle Condition

You will receive the vehicle in clean condition with a full tank of fuel. You are responsible for returning the vehicle in the same condition, except for normal wear and tear.

Note: The vehicle must be returned to the agreed location. One-way rentals between certain locations are available at additional cost.

7. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination of the rental agreement, additional charges, and legal action:

  • Operating the vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any intoxicating substances
  • Using the vehicle for illegal activities, including but not limited to smuggling, transportation of illegal substances, or commission of crimes
  • Using the vehicle for racing, pace making, speed testing, or driver training
  • Using the vehicle to tow or push any vehicle, trailer, or other object without prior written authorization
  • Driving off-road or on unpaved roads (except SUVs specifically authorized for such use)
  • Using the vehicle for commercial passenger transportation (rideshare services like Uber or Lyft)
  • Transporting hazardous materials, explosives, or flammable substances
  • Smoking or vaping inside the vehicle (a $250 cleaning fee will apply if violated)
  • Transporting pets without prior authorization and payment of pet fee (service animals exempt)
  • Allowing unauthorized persons to drive the vehicle
Important: Violation of any prohibited activities will void all insurance coverage and may result in additional charges up to the full value of the vehicle.

8. Damages and Liability

You are responsible for any damage to or loss of the vehicle during the rental period, regardless of fault, except for normal wear and tear.

8.1 Accident Procedure

In the event of an accident, you must:

  1. Ensure the safety of all persons involved
  2. Contact local police to file a report
  3. Contact DriveRent immediately at our 24/7 emergency number
  4. Exchange insurance and contact information with other parties
  5. Take photos of the accident scene, vehicle damage, and all relevant documents
  6. Do not admit fault or liability to any party

8.2 Damage Assessment

DriveRent will assess any damage to the vehicle upon return. You will be charged for repair costs, loss of use during repairs, and diminished value of the vehicle. The security deposit may be used to cover these charges.
