Service FAQs

Common questions about our rental services

Can I modify or cancel my reservation? +

Yes, you can modify or cancel your reservation free of charge up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time. Changes or cancellations within 24 hours may incur a fee. You can manage your reservation through your online account or by contacting our customer service.

What happens if I return the car late? +

We provide a 59-minute grace period after your scheduled return time. If you return the car more than one hour late, additional charges will apply at the standard daily rate. If you know you'll be late, please contact us as soon as possible to extend your rental period.

Do you offer one-way rentals? +

Yes, we offer one-way rentals between most locations. Additional fees may apply depending on the distance between pickup and drop-off locations. Please check availability and pricing when making your reservation or contact our customer service for specific one-way rental options.

Can I rent a car without a credit card? +

While we prefer credit cards for security deposits, we also accept debit cards and certain digital payment methods. Additional verification may be required for non-credit card payments. Contact our customer service team to discuss alternative payment options for your rental.

What is included in the rental price? +

Our base rental price includes the vehicle, basic insurance coverage, and standard roadside assistance. Additional charges may apply for optional services like GPS, child seats, additional drivers, and premium insurance. All applicable taxes and fees are clearly displayed during the booking process.
