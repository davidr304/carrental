Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to rent a car? +

You'll need a valid driver's license, a credit card in the renter's name, and proof of insurance. International renters may need an International Driving Permit along with their home country license.

What is your cancellation policy? +

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your pickup time. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a fee. Please check our full cancellation policy in the terms and conditions.

Do you offer one-way rentals? +

Yes, we offer one-way rentals between most of our locations. Additional fees may apply depending on the pickup and drop-off locations. Contact our support team for specific pricing.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), debit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency payments (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT).

Is there a minimum age requirement? +

The minimum age to rent a car is 21 years. Drivers under 25 may be subject to a young driver surcharge. Some luxury or specialty vehicles may have higher age requirements.
